One sentence, at most 280 characters, that brings back a childhood memory — entered in the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363).

## The entry

> When the rice cooker clicked at five o'clock, my grandmother lifted the lid with a folded dish towel, and the steam smelled like every summer I have ever missed.

161 characters, one sentence. Grounded in concrete sensations the house judge weighs first: the rice cooker's click, a folded dish towel, steam — and the warm ache of summers missed.

## How it works

`main.py` validates the sentence against the competition gate (UTF-8 text, 1–280 characters, one line, terminal punctuation, a concrete sensory detail and a memory anchor) and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, then reads it back and verifies the bytes match.
